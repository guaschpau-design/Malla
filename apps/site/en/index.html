What it does

  • Positions

    Everyone in the group, on the map. You share your position when you want to, and pause or hide it when you do not.

  • SOS

    One button that alerts the whole group with your position. Over radio it repeats by itself until someone answers.

  • Group chat

    Short messages, because over radio every byte counts. What you write offline goes out as soon as there is a path.

  • Marks on the map

    Meeting points, hazards, areas and routes. The whole group sees them and anyone can correct them.

  • Alerts from the coordinator

    A critical alert stays on screen until each person confirms it, and the coordinator sees who has.

  • Offline map

    Download your area on a quiet day and the map is still there when there is no internet.

Where it stands

Teselia is a young project. We would rather say what works and what does not than promise.

Over the internet
Works. It is what you use when you open the app.
Over LoRa radio
Built and in field testing with MeshCore radios. Until that is done, we recommend no model.
Command post
Built and being tested. There are instructions to set one up.
In the browser
Android, iPhone, tablet and computer. It goes on the home screen like an app. Connecting a radio needs Chrome or Edge.
Android and iPhone apps
In development. They will bring background position, alerts with the app closed and radio on iPhone.
Code
Public, under the European EUPL licence. Anyone can review it or run their own server.

Start on a quiet day

An emergency is no time to learn an app. This takes ten minutes.

  1. Open Teselia and choose your name

    No email, phone number or password. Add it to your home screen.

  2. Create the group or join with the code

    Whoever creates it coordinates and invites with a link or a QR code. Whoever has it, gets in.

  3. Download the map of your area

    On Wi-Fi, from “Offline maps”. Then run a test together.

Get Teselia

It opens from the browser at teselia.eu/app, with no store in between, and updates by itself.

Open Teselia

Add it to your home screen

  • Android, with Chrome: menu ⋮, “Add to Home screen”.
  • iPhone, with Safari: Share button, “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Computer, with Chrome or Edge: the install icon in the address bar.

What Teselia does not do

  • It asks for no account, email or phone number.
  • It does not read your messages: they are encrypted with a key only the group has.
  • It shows no ads and keeps no record of who connects.
  • It does not replace 112. If you can call, call.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

No, and it will not. Only the radios, if you decide to use them.

Do I need a radio?

No. With internet, Teselia works with nothing else. The radio is for when there is no coverage.

Which radios can I use?

MeshCore radios running the “Companion Bluetooth” firmware. We are testing them in the field and, until that is done, we recommend no model. We will publish it here.

Does it work on iPhone?

Over the internet, the same as on Android. Safari does not allow connecting a radio by Bluetooth; the App Store app will, and a command post works meanwhile.

Who is behind it?

A citizen-led free software project under the European EUPL licence. The code, the decisions and what is pending are on GitHub.
