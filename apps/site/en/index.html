Open the app

Your group, on the map. With coverage or without.

Teselia is a free app that lets a group of people see where everyone is, message each other, mark what matters on the map and call for help. It works over the internet and, when there is no coverage, through small pocket radios.

Who is it for?

For any group that needs to coordinate on the ground, especially when things get hard: a blackout, a flood, a wildfire or just an area without coverage.

🏘️

Neighbours and associations

Organise the neighbourhood during an emergency and know who needs help.

🦺

Volunteers and civil protection

Split up areas, mark meeting points and warn of hazards as they happen.

⛰️

Mountains and the outdoors

Lose nobody where phones have no signal.

👨‍👩‍👧

Families and friends

A simple plan to find each other if communications ever fail.

What you can do

Everything on one screen: the map. No complicated menus.

📍

See where your people are

Everyone in the group appears on the map. You decide when to share your position, and you can pause or hide it.

💬

Talk to the group

One chat for the whole group, which also travels by radio when there is no internet.

🆘

Call for help

An SOS button alerts the whole group with your position. Over radio, it repeats until someone answers.

🗺️

Mark the map

Meeting points, hazards, areas and routes that the whole group sees at once.

📥

Offline maps

Download your area before you go and the map keeps working without internet.

📣

Coordinator alerts

Coordinators can send important alerts and see who has confirmed them.

How it works

Teselia uses the best path available at every moment. You do not have to choose: the app switches on its own.

🌐

1. With internet

📱 ⇄ ☁️ ⇄ 📱

Like any messaging app. Messages pass, encrypted, through our server in Spain, which cannot read them.

📻

2. Without internet, by radio

📱 ⇢ 📻 · · · 📻 ⇢ 📱

Connect your phone by Bluetooth to a pocket LoRa radio. The radios pass messages to each other over several kilometres, with no phone masts.

🏕️

3. Command post

New · being tested
📱📱 ⇄ 💻 ⇄ 📻

A laptop or a Raspberry Pi creates its own Wi-Fi at the scene. Phones join it, even without a radio, and the post sends everything by radio and, once internet is back, to the rest of the group.

Does it work on my device?

Over the internet, Teselia works on any phone, tablet or computer with a current browser. The radio needs Bluetooth in the browser, which for now only Chrome and Edge have.

Device Over the internet With LoRa radio
Android phone or tablet, with Chrome Yes Yes
iPhone or iPad Yes Not yet with Safari. It will come with the App Store app. Meanwhile, it can be tried with the Bluefy browser.
Windows, Mac or Linux computer, with Chrome or Edge Yes Yes
Computer with Firefox or Safari Yes No. Use Chrome or Edge for the radio.

Supported radios

Teselia uses MeshCore radios running the “Companion Bluetooth” firmware. These are the ones we test:

Radio What it is like Best for
SenseCAP T1000-E Card-sized, water-resistant, battery lasts for days. The one we recommend to start with.
SenseCAP MeshTracker X1 Compact, with the newest radio chip. An alternative to the T1000-E.
LilyGO T-Deck Plus Screen, keyboard and GPS. Anyone who also wants to use it on its own, with other firmware.
Elecrow ThinkNode M9 Screen, keyboard and GPS. Like the T-Deck. Its factory firmware must be replaced.

Radios are set up once from a computer with the official MeshCore flasher. The radio guide explains it step by step. In Europe they use the licence-free 868 MHz band.

Get ready in five steps

An emergency is no time to learn an app. Spend half an hour on a quiet day.

  1. Open Teselia and choose your name. No email, phone number or password needed.
  2. Create a group and invite your people. Share the link or the QR code through whatever channel you already use.
  3. Download the map of your area. In “Offline maps”, so it works even without internet.
  4. If you can, get a few radios. One per person or per household is enough to talk without coverage.
  5. Run a test together. Send a message in airplane mode with Bluetooth on and check that it arrives by radio.

Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Teselia is free and will stay free. You only pay for radios, if you want to use them.

Do I need a radio to use Teselia?

No. With internet it works without one. The radio keeps you in touch when there is no coverage.

How far does the radio reach?

It depends on the terrain: from about a kilometre in a city to tens of kilometres between high places with a clear view. Radios repeat each other's messages, so the more there are, the further messages travel.

Why are radio messages short and slower?

LoRa radio reaches very far in exchange for sending very little data. Teselia puts urgent things first: an SOS goes out before a position or a chat message.

Can I talk to people using the MeshCore app?

You can read their messages in the chat's MeshCore tab. Your Teselia group's messages are seen only by the people in the group.

Does it replace emergency services?

No. If you can, always call 112. Teselia helps your group coordinate; it does not alert emergency services.

Who is behind it?

A citizen-led open-source project under the European EUPL licence. Anyone can review the code, suggest improvements or run their own server.
