Capabilities

A common operating picture.

Every member sees the same thing, on the same map, at the same time: where the team is, what is happening and what has been decided.

  1. Team tracking

    Every member's position in real time. Each person decides when to share it, pause it or hide it.

  2. SOS with acknowledgement

    A call for help, with its type and position, reaches the whole team at once. Whoever sent it sees who has received it.

  3. Team messaging

    Short messages, built to travel over radio too. Whatever is written without coverage goes out as soon as there is a link.

  4. Tasks and reports

    Coordinators hand out tasks to people or teams, with their place on the map. Everyone reports what they find with situation reports.

  5. Terrain marking

    Sectors, meeting points, hazards and routes, shared with and editable by the whole team.

  6. Offline maps

    The map of the area of operations, downloaded in advance. Still there when the network is not.

Connectivity

Keeps working when the network goes down.

The same app and the same data, whatever infrastructure is available. Tesel uses whichever link it finds and syncs everything when the connection returns.

Internet

With mobile data or wifi, through Tesel's server or your own. The server relays the encrypted data without being able to read it.

Device Server Team

LoRa radio mesh

Each phone pairs over Bluetooth with a pocket radio. The radios relay messages between them over kilometres, with no phone masts involved.

Device Radio Mesh

Automatic bridge

A phone with a radio and coverage joins both networks with nothing to set up: what arrives by radio goes up to the server, and what the server has goes out by radio. Whoever has no coverage stays up to date.

Who it is for

For teams that work in the field.

  • Civil protection

    Volunteer groups and local services, in emergencies and at planned events.

  • Search and rescue

    Sector searches, with the ground divided up and every team in view.

  • NGOs and humanitarian aid

    Teams deployed where infrastructure fails or does not exist.

  • Events and operations

    Security, medical and staff at races, festivals and large gatherings.

  • Mountains and expeditions

    Groups beyond coverage who need to know about each other.

Deployment

Operational in three steps.

No installs, no sign-ups. What takes time is practice, and that is best done early.

  1. Create the team

    The coordinator creates the group and invites with a link or a QR code. Each member only picks a name.

  2. Prepare the area

    Download the map of the area of operations over wifi and, if you use radios, pair them with your phones.

  3. Run a drill

    Practise positions, SOS and tasks on a quiet day. An emergency is not the time to learn a new tool.

Start now

It runs in the browser.

On any phone, tablet or computer, at tesel.eu/app. No app stores, and it updates itself.

Open Tesel

Install it as an app

  • Android, with Chrome: menu ⋮, “Add to Home screen”.
  • iPhone, with Safari: Share button, “Add to Home Screen”.
  • Computer, with Chrome or Edge: the install icon in the address bar.

Native apps for Android and iOS are in development: background location, SOS with the app closed, and radio on iPhone too.

Security

Private by design.

  • No accounts

    No email, no phone number, no password. Each member only picks a name.

  • End-to-end encryption

    Only the team holds the key. The server cannot read anything.

  • No tracking

    No ads, no analytics and no logs of who connects.

  • Open source

    European EUPL licence. Anyone can audit it or run their own server.

Tesel does not replace 112. If you can call, call.

Questions

Before you deploy it.

Does it cost anything?

No, and it will not. Only the radios, if you decide to use them.

Do we need radios?

No. With coverage, Tesel needs nothing else. Radios are for when there is none.

Which radios are supported?

LoRa radios running MeshCore with its “Companion Bluetooth” firmware. We are validating them in the field and will publish recommended models here.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, over the internet. Safari does not allow Bluetooth radios; the native app will.

Can we run our own server?

Yes. The server is free software and runs on a modest computer or in the cloud.

Who is behind it?

A citizen-led free software project, under the European EUPL-1.2 licence.
