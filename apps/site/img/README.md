# Images

`hero.jpg` and `hero-tall.jpg`: hikers with headlamps at blue hour, by Maël Balland on Unsplash (<https://unsplash.com/photos/pG5_vXaRj9s>), under the Unsplash licence. Cropped and slightly darkened for the home page.

`app-tablet`, `app-sos` and `app-phone`: screenshots of the real app, staged with a fictional team
in Valencia by the simulator (`pnpm --filter @tesel/simulator screenshots`, see `tools/simulator`):
`tablet-chat.png`, `tablet-map.png` and `phone-map.png`, resized to 1800, 1400 and 640 pixels wide
and saved as WebP and JPEG. The base map is Tesel's own (OpenStreetMap data, Protomaps). Map data © OpenStreetMap
contributors, ODbL.
